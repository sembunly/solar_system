# Planet texture credits

All maps are bundled locally in `public/textures`, so the scene does not fetch image assets from third-party servers at runtime. They are equirectangular maps loaded with Three.js `TextureLoader`.

## Planets and the Moon

The 2K planetary maps, the Sun map, and the 2K Moon map come from [Solar System Scope's texture pack](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/). Solar System Scope says the maps are based on NASA imagery and elevation data and distributes them under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit: Solar System Scope.

## Modeled moons

The Moon texture uses the Solar System Scope map above. The other moon maps are NASA/JPL/Caltech/USGS spacecraft mosaics from the [JPL Solar System Simulator texture archive](https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/):

- Io, Europa, and Ganymede: Voyager/Galileo maps from [JPL's Jupiter texture page](https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/jupiter.html).
- Phobos and Deimos: Viking/USGS maps from [JPL's Mars texture page](https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/mars.html).
- Enceladus: Voyager/USGS map from [JPL's Saturn texture page](https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/saturn.html).
- Miranda: Voyager/USGS map from [JPL's Uranus texture page](https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/uranus.html).
- Triton: Voyager/USGS map from [JPL's Neptune texture page](https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/neptune.html).
- Titan: Cassini ISS map PIA08346, credited to [NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/map-of-titan-december-2006/). The map frame was cropped and resized for spherical mapping.
